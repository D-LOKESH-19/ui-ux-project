# 5. Ideation and Concept Selection

*Stage: Ideate*

## Rough sketches or rapid ideas

Suggested rapid ideas to sketch: number-only screen; ring around the screen as a trend gauge; full-screen colour per alert level; haptic pattern per level; crown-button control for exercise; sleep screen with one huge button; day and night themes.

## At least three distinct concepts

- **Concept A, Glance-first:** one huge number and arrow, everything else hidden. Very fast, little context.
- **Concept B, Graph-first:** the trend graph is the home screen. Rich information, but cramped on a tiny screen.
- **Concept C, Adaptive urgency:** a glance screen that changes with context (day, exercise, sleep) and with four alert levels, with haptics and escalation.

## Chosen concept and reason for selection

**Concept C, adaptive urgency.** It is the only concept that addresses all three core challenges: the tiny screen (a glance-first home), urgency tiers (four levels with distinct icons, words and haptics), and context awareness (exercise and sleep modes, Day and Night themes). It also targets the best-evidenced problems: alarm fatigue [1] and missed night alerts [4]. The cost is added complexity, so the scope is limited to the three critical tasks.
