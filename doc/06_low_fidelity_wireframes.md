## Annotations explaining layout and interaction decisions

- **One message per screen.** The graph is a swipe away from the glance screen.
- **The number and arrow are the largest items,** because the first question is "Am I okay and where am I heading?" [7]
- **One primary action per alert card,** large and low on the screen.
- **Exercise mode supports a hardware button or crown,** since sweaty or gloved hands make touch unreliable.
- **Sleep mode keeps the screen dim,** with a single large target for a half-awake user.
- **Stale data has its own screen,** because trust depends on knowing sensor data can lag or drop [3][10].
